Built by Players, for Players

DartVault is an independent darts gear company run by people who throw every week.

Our Story

DartVault started the way most good darts stories do — at the oche, arguing about barrel grips and flight shapes after a league night. We were tired of paying premium prices for average gear, so we went straight to the workshops that manufacture for the big brands and started sourcing our own.

What We Do

We curate and stock a focused range of steel tip and soft tip darts, flights, shafts, boards, cases and accessories. Every product we list is thrown, tested and approved by players on our team before it makes it into the catalog.

Our Promise

Honest specs, fair prices and fast worldwide shipping. If something isn't right, our 30-day guarantee has you covered.

What We Stand For

Three simple rules that guide every decision we make.

Quality First

Every item is hand-checked and tested at the oche before it ships.

Fair Prices

Factory-direct sourcing keeps tournament-grade gear affordable.

Players First

Real advice from league players — before and after you buy.
